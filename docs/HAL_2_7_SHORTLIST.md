# HAL 2.7 Shortlist

`quote_current()` remains the complete internal quote universe.

`quote_shortlist()` is client-facing and:
1. removes verified plans that fail any MUST-HAVE requirement;
2. prioritises verified full matches;
3. keeps unverified other-provider options as indicative alternatives;
4. encourages provider diversity;
5. attaches client-facing card metadata.

Example: routine maternity required.
Morgan Price Standard, Standard Plus and Comprehensive are removed from the shortlist.
Premium and Elite remain.

Internal evidence fields are not rendered on the quote cards.

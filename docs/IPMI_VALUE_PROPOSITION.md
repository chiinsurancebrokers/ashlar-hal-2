# HAL — Comprehensive IPMI Value Proposition

For clients seeking comprehensive/high-end private medical protection, HAL leads with
the case for IPMI.

Key differentiators:
- outpatient specialist/diagnostic access;
- broad provider choice and international networks;
- higher or less fragmented limits;
- prescribed medicines where verified;
- mental-health benefits;
- complementary therapies where verified;
- dental / optical / wellness;
- maternity;
- rehabilitation;
- evacuation / repatriation;
- surgeon/specialist fee reimbursement design;
- portability, international assistance and direct billing.

## Physician fee gap
A hospital admission can be insured while the member still faces a balance if the
contract applies a surgeon/physician fee schedule or sublimit. HAL compares the actual
local and international policy terms.

For Morgan Price Evolution EU 2026, the supplied Table of Benefits shows hospital
surgeon/anaesthetist/consultant/physician fees as Full refund across the five plans,
subject to annual maximum and policy terms.

## Vitamins
Do not market vitamins as a generic IPMI benefit. Prescribed medicines may be covered
where verified; vitamins/supplements require explicit policy support.

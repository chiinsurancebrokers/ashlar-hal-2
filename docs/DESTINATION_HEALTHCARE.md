# Destination Healthcare Intelligence

Each curated country profile is separate from insurance-product data.

## Greece v1

Current evidence uses OECD / State of Health in the EU / European Observatory
sources. HAL is instructed to present both strengths and constraints.

Examples:
- universal core-service eligibility,
- life expectancy above the OECD average,
- low reported satisfaction with availability of quality care,
- high unmet-need indicators,
- substantial out-of-pocket expenditure,
- access and waiting-list pressures.

## Important denominator rule

Different official datasets can produce different unmet-needs figures because the
denominators and survey definitions differ. HAL must retain the denominator note and
must not present the figures as contradictory.
